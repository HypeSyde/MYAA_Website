Founded in Poole, Dorset, Sunseeker has become one of the most recognised names in luxury yachting — its yachts as celebrated on the silver screen as they are on the world's great cruising grounds.

From the sociable Manhattan and tri-deck Yacht ranges, to the light-filled Ocean series and the adrenaline of the legendary Predator, every Sunseeker is engineered around the way its owners truly live on the water.

As the official Western Australian dealer, MYA brings the full Sunseeker range to Fremantle — with expert guidance, sea trials and after-sales care at every stage of ownership.
