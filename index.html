Our Story

A trusted name on Fremantle's waterfront

Motoryachts Australasia is a premier luxury yacht sales and marine services provider based in Fremantle, Western Australia. Founded in 2021, we are a proud member of the DCH Marine Network and an authorised Mercury dealership — a name swiftly established as one to trust across the Australian and international marine industry.

Specialising in both new and pre-owned luxury yachts, we pair high-level technical expertise with transparent, reliable and deeply personal service at every stage of ownership.

More than 80 years of combined leadership and experience — deep knowledge, refined craftsmanship and an unwavering dedication to quality. — The MYA Team
