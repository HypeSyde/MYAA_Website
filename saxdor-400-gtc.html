The Saxdor 400 GTC is the formidable flagship of the range — a 40-foot enclosed coupé that fuses supercar aesthetics with genuine cruising practicality. Its fully glazed, air-conditioned saloon opens to the elements through sliding doors and a vast electric sunroof.

Below decks, two separate cabins and a full heads make the 400 GTC a credible weekender for two couples, while the expansive cockpit, foredeck lounge and broad bathing platform host the whole crew by day.

With up to triple Mercury outboards, the 400 GTC pairs breathtaking acceleration and a 45-knot top end with the efficiency and easy maintenance of modern outboard power.

  • Enclosed, air-conditioned saloon with panoramic glazing
  • Two private cabins and a full heads compartment
  • Electric sunroof and sliding glass terrace doors
  • Up to triple Mercury outboard installation
  • Expansive bathing platform and foredeck lounge
