The Predator 55 brings the unmistakable attitude of the performance range into a more compact, owner-operator package. A sliding sunroof and opening aft glazing let the outside in, while a single flowing main deck blends helm, galley and saloon into one sociable space.

Below, three cabins — led by a full-beam midships owner's suite — provide genuine overnight comfort, and a clever aft layout maximises lounging on deck.

With Volvo IPS pod drives, the Predator 55 is as easy to dock single-handed as it is exhilarating to open up offshore.

  • Sliding sunroof over a single-level main deck
  • Volvo IPS joystick handling and efficient cruising
  • Full-beam owner's suite amidships
  • Hydraulic bathing platform and aft sunpad
  • Signature Predator performance styling
