The Saxdor 200 is the boat that put the brand on the map — a compact, sharply-styled day boat that delivers big-boat looks and a thrilling ride at a fraction of the cost. Its deep-V hull and single outboard make it efficient, trailerable and effortlessly quick.

Available in open Sport and console Pro layouts, the 200 offers clever, configurable seating, a concealed heads option and generous sunbathing for a boat of its size.

It is the ideal first step into the Saxdor world — and a hugely capable tender or coastal companion for a larger yacht.

  • Award-winning, scaled-down Saxdor styling
  • Efficient single Mercury outboard power
  • Configurable seating and sunbathing
  • Optional concealed heads compartment
  • Trailerable, easy to own and run
