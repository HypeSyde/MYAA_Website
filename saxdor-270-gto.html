The Saxdor 270 GTO brings open Gran Turismo styling to a nimble 27-foot platform. A sociable cockpit, a forward sun-lounge and a clever convertible layout make it perfect for spirited day trips and the occasional overnight.

A compact cabin with berth and stowage tucks beneath the console, while the deep, dry-riding hull and single or twin Mercury outboards keep the experience pure and exhilarating.

Easy to handle and beautifully finished, the 270 GTO is a Saxdor for those who value performance and clean, contemporary design.

  • Open GTO cockpit with convertible seating
  • Forward sun-lounge over a compact cabin
  • Single or twin Mercury outboard options
  • Deep-V hull for a dry, confident ride
  • Trailerable, sporty weekender
