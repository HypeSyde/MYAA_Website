The 90 Ocean Enclosed represents the pinnacle of Sunseeker's celebrated Ocean range. Where the open 90 Ocean offers an al-fresco sky helm, the Enclosed variant wraps that same commanding upper deck in a fully glazed superstructure — creating a climate-controlled sky lounge bathed in natural light, usable in every season and every latitude.

Below, an expansive main deck flows from a forward galley and formal dining through to a full-beam owner's stateroom, four guest cabins and generous crew quarters aft. Floor-to-ceiling glazing and a fold-down balcony bring the sea into the heart of the accommodation, while the hydraulic bathing platform launches a tender and toys with ease.

Powered by twin MAN V12 engines, the 90 Ocean Enclosed marries genuine long-range capability with the effortless performance that defines the marque — a true owner-operator superyacht for Western Australian waters and beyond.

  • Fully enclosed, air-conditioned sky lounge with sliding glass aft doors
  • Full-beam owner's stateroom with private study and walk-in dressing room
  • Foredeck cockpit with sunpads, seating and concealed sun awning
  • Hydraulic submersible bathing platform and dedicated tender garage
  • Four en-suite guest cabins finished in hand-selected timber veneers
