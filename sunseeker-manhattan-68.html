The Manhattan 68 is Sunseeker's quintessential flybridge cruiser — a yacht designed around family, friends and the social rhythm of life on the water. Its expansive flybridge, sheltered by an optional hardtop, offers dining, sun-lounging and a second helm with commanding views.

On the main deck, a galley-forward layout opens the saloon and cockpit into one continuous entertaining space. Four cabins below, including a full-beam owner's stateroom, sleep eight in comfort.

Easy to handle, generous in volume and timelessly styled, the Manhattan 68 is the natural choice for owners who want flybridge versatility without sacrificing Sunseeker's signature elegance.

  • Expansive flybridge with second helm, bar and dining
  • Galley-forward main deck for open saloon-to-cockpit living
  • Full-beam owner's stateroom with abundant natural light
  • Hydraulic bathing platform for tender and toys
  • Refined, family-friendly four-cabin layout
